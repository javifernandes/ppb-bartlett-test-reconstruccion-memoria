At dawn, a woman arrived at a village by the river carrying a basket covered with blue leaves. She said she had come from the mountains and was looking for the house where the stones sang during the rain.

An old man warned her not to cross the bridge until three canoes had passed, because the river was still dreaming. The woman waited, but she saw only two. Then she left the basket on the riverbank and crossed.

When she reached the other side, she heard a whistle coming from inside the basket. When she opened it, she found a small fish with white feathers. The fish spoke the old man's name and then became still.

That night, it began to rain. From the village, the people could hear the stones singing, but the woman did not return. The next morning, they found the empty basket beneath the bridge.
